# Independent Agent Wallet Merger Integrity Audit

**Verdict: FAIL — confidence in complete research preservation is not justified.**

Audit batch: core-concept reconciliation and selected atomic findings. Source package dated 13 October 2026. Prepared 14 October 2026, Asia/Dhaka. This is a fidelity audit, not a security certification, external fact-check, competition ranking or winner selection.

## 1. Executive integrity summary

The synthesis retains substantial useful work, but it does not reliably preserve every material source mechanism. Confirmed examples change the financial model, authorization boundary or execution state: batch payroll becomes streaming payroll; evidence-assessed indemnity claims become parametric claims; period budgets become liability reserves; self-monitoring becomes independent monitoring; behavioral drift becomes permission-configuration drift. These are material differences, even when the replacement is a safer design.

The current batch independently extracts **249 core concept/contribution records** and **33 supplemental atomic findings** from R001–R014. Every extracted record has a traceable decision. Among the core records, **14 are incorrectly merged, 3 are misrepresented, 75 are partially preserved, 125 are preserved through a defensible merge, and 32 are uncertain**. Twenty-two core records have high-priority discrepancies. The supplemental register adds detail and overlaps some parent concepts; its entries must not be counted as independent discoveries automatically.

**This is not a completed exhaustive A–L atomic audit.** All 14 supplied exports and all 5,556 source blocks were parsed and indexed, and the recoverable core-concept batch was reconciled. The surrounding problem catalogues, prior-art tables, architecture variants, experiments, citations and prose still require an exhaustive atomic closure pass. Five reports also contain source gaps that this package cannot recover. An overall full-corpus semantic coverage percentage is therefore unavailable. The user’s strict completeness requirement is not met.

The source gaps were already disclosed by the synthesis in section 2. They are not proof that its author deleted complete original material. Conversely, retaining raw source text or a crosswalk label does not establish meaningful preservation in the normalized technical definitions.

## 8. Incorrect merges and misrepresentations

All 14 core false-merge records and all 3 core misrepresentation records are listed individually in the matrix. The primary mechanisms are:

- **Payroll:** six confirmed source records mapped to streams are actually batch/pay-run or work-confirmation payments: R001 F03, R003 C20, R005 C10, R009 C17, R012 C04 and R013 C09 are confirmed false merges in this batch; the source-damaged R006 payroll fragment remains uncertain. Do not count the uncertain fragment as a seventh confirmed error.
- **Claims:** R008 ClaimFlow requires indemnity assessment. R011’s generic claims sentence is too thin to certify parametric insurance.
- **Payment versus escrow:** R008 GlobalPay pays after a delivery signal; a prefunded escrow adds custody and release states. R011’s GitHub PR milestone does not establish physical-goods escrow.
- **Budgets:** cost-center/task envelopes are not authenticated-obligation reserves. Forecast buffers are another variant.
- **Monitoring:** R010 C08 explicitly self-detects compromise; IDEA-009 replaces it with an independent watcher. R012 C11 monitors agent behavior; IDEA-049 monitors installed permissions. Preserve each original and label the redesign.
- **Repayment:** R010 C14 optimizes by interest rate and deadline within a budget; IDEA-038 follows owner-fixed creditor seniority. Their allocation objectives and autonomy differ.
- **Other wrong mappings:** R008 Guardian is treasury proposal authorization, not incident response; R010 C10 belongs to subscription cancellation; R011 C22 is runtime trace/MEV analysis, not merely an attack benchmark or contract-reading assistant.

These corrections preserve valid synthesis work. They do not require adopting an unsafe source proposal or undoing justified criticism.

## 9. Security and feasibility distinctions to restore

Restore the source mechanisms with their limitations intact: delayed cancellation queues; named escrow quorum membership; independent counterparty acceptance/attestation gates; two-model signatures plus deterministic validation; streaming deposit/buffer and liquidation accounting; insurance-pool liquidity and correlated-event exposure; browser/token-metadata attack inputs; fee-bounded replacement of stuck transactions; and distinct gas sponsorship versus gas replenishment.

The synthesis already preserves many strong controls: trusted intent versus untrusted documents, exact-byte authorization, aggregate exposure, receipt lifecycle, timeout/idempotency handling, independent owner recovery, privacy boundaries and adversarial testing. R014’s explicit proposed-versus-executed test labels are also retained. They should not be described as lost.

Specific omitted or weakened feasibility tests include R008’s delivery-oracle integration experiment (L03468), R010’s attestation bootstrap dependency (L04088), and chain-to-chain conformance versus backend-to-backend conformance (R010 C21). A transfer hash does not validate a shipping oracle, human authorization role or mandate parser. This is a fidelity finding, not a claim that the untested source implementation works.

## 11. Claim and citation integrity

The synthesis’s claim log contains valuable, appropriately cautious corrections. It separates proposed prototypes from executed tests, disputes guarantees from key isolation or simulation, qualifies reputation and novelty, and records source incompleteness. Disagreeing with a source overclaim is not automatically a merger defect when the original position and correction remain explicit.

Two attribution repairs are needed. **CL-002** groups R002 with a certainty-versus-mainnet disagreement even though R002 explicitly asks for written testnet confirmation (L00501/L00551/L00809). **CL-022** uses an all-report attribution for a daily-cap/lifetime-loss error although R014 L05371 states a conditional session ceiling and its defeating assumptions. Preserve each source’s actual position rather than implying all named reports made the criticized claim. CL-034 is already labeled a *potential reading* of R010; this audit does not falsely accuse it of being a fabricated quotation.

The reference parser has a reproducible defect. `supplied_urls` has **223 records**, but **12 R013 fields** contain trailing quotation marks and **four join two URLs**. Reparsing yields **227 URL occurrences, 181 unique URL strings**. Comparing source occurrences with reparsed ledger occurrences gives zero missing and zero extra. This is a structured-reference corruption/counting defect, not evidence that URL text was deleted. `data/independent_url_inventory.json` supplies corrected separate records with original anchors. External destination availability, bibliographic identity and factual support remain unverified here.

Product mention registers and raw table rows preserve access to source assertions but do not certify their technical interpretation. Bibliographic weaknesses inherited from the source are distinguished from malformed URLs introduced by ledger extraction. No new literature review or external capability claims were added to this audit.
